Nickunj Creator AI is a content management, publishing, and analytics application developed by Nickunj03. The application is initially intended for personal use to manage and analyze Pinterest content.

When you authorize Nickunj Creator AI to connect to a Pinterest account, the application may access information permitted through Pinterest's authorization system, including account information, Pins, Boards, and analytics data required for the application's functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Pinterest is used to:
